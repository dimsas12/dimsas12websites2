1. The thesis: why DraftKings is wrong

DraftKings' ladder (2+ −250, 3+ +120, 4+ +310) prices about 2.6 saves, which is Courtois's all-competitions average last season (2.86). That average is dragged down by La Liga games against small teams (0–1 saves against Levante, Valencia, Osasuna). In the Champions League Madrid play more openly and concede more: in 14 CL games (11 last season, 3 this season) Courtois always made 3 or more saves.
