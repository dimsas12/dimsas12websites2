The short version

  1. His edge is small per bet and comes from price and volume. He wins 61% at odds where 54% breaks even. That is about 7 points of edge, spread over ~7.5 bets a day at a flat 1 unit. 21 of his 52 days were losing days. September went 78-38 (+29.2u), October 155-109 (+23.1u): the hit rate fell as the volume more than doubled.
  2. 98% of his bets sit between 1.65 and 2.50, median 1.86. His best band is −125 to −149 (1.67–1.80): 67-30, 69%, ROI +21%. Our odds range already matches his. The difference is what we put inside it.
  3. He bets opportunity, not efficiency. His best markets are counted by workload: MLB pitcher outs (how long the manager leaves him in) 16-4, NFL receiving yards (targets) 34-21, NBA threes for high-volume shooters 7-1, assists for primary ball-handlers. Markets that depend on shooting efficiency are flat or losing: NBA points 12-11, NFL receptions 9-9, CFB rushing 3-6, NBA PRA 2-3.
  4. His NBA picks were not "obvious" plays. Before the game, the median pick had gone over his line in only 56% of last season's games, and the line sat just 7% inside his baseline. His best bucket was last-season hit rate 50–59% (13-6, +6.4u). Picks below 50% lost (7-10, −3.8u). He takes lines that look fair to most people and are slightly mispriced. Lines that look like locks are already shaded by the book.
  5. Opening week was his best NBA spot (21-11, +7.7u). Books set the first lines with little current data, and he trusted last season's full sample over preseason noise. Later, fading slow starters lost (4-7) and chasing hot new roles broke even (6-5).
  6. "A teammate is out" was not an edge for him (5-5). Books move those lines within minutes. Role changes only pay if you get the number before it moves.
  7. Mostly overs (289 vs 39 unders), but his unders also won (24-15). He did not ban unders. He just rarely found them.
  8. Tennis moneyline favourites were his best sport by ROI: 20-7, +34%. We don't bet tennis yet.

Changes I am making to the daily plan:
  • Rank markets by opportunity. Assists for primary handlers, rebounds for starting bigs, threes for high-volume shooters, R+A and P+A first. Points props only with a very strong thesis.
  • Replace the hard "70% hit rate in the last 5–10" test with a larger sample: last season + this season, at least 10 games, hit rate ≥ 58%, and the projection ≥ 8% past the line. A 70% hit rate in 5 games is usually already in the price.
  • Target odds −125 to −150 (1.67–1.80) first, his strongest band and our "can't lose" band.
  • In the first two weeks of a season, weight last season's numbers more than the first few games.
  • Treat "teammate out" as a reason only if the DraftKings line has not already moved for it.
  • Keep flat stakes (0.75–1%). His results come from many bets with a small edge, not from big confident bets.
